# Wave 2 - the tablet and the Android app (v1.3)

**Status:** shipped in v1.3 (`72deb0d` and the follow-ups).
**Kanso:** card 41 (Done).
**Goal:** the drawing instrument has to work with a finger on a tablet.

## Why

UPIC was a pen-and-tablet instrument. A phone-sized mouse-driven layout
is not the same thing: the canvas, the toolbars and the hit targets have
to be laid out in density-independent pixels, and the drawing has to feel
right under a thumb.

## What was decided

- **Everything is in dp (logical pixels).** JUCE 8 works in logical
  pixels everywhere; the window peers multiply the bounds by the display
  scale. So the layout code is written once and is correct on a phone, a
  tablet and a desktop.
- **The layout is one responsive grid**, not several screens. The
  selftest checks the layout at six sizes (1280x800 down to 360x640) for
  pairwise non-overlap and for the named controls keeping a usable size.
- **Touch is a first-class input.** One finger draws or selects, a pinch
  zooms time and pitch, two fingers pan, a long press opens the context
  menu. The mouse path stays for the desktop.
- **Immersive fullscreen, re-asserted.** The app hides the system bars
  and keeps them hidden: a swipe, a focus loss, a volume panel or a
  rotation all clear the hide request, so the app re-asserts it on a
  timer (`AndroidBars.h`).
- **The Android project is a normal Gradle project** in `Builds/Android`,
  generated by Projucer from `UPICuitous.jucer` and built in Android
  Studio. The JUCE CMake build produces `libUPICuitous.so`; Gradle
  packages it into the APK.
- **Two product flavours** (`debug_`, `release_`) because the JUCE CMake
  build needs a different `JUCE_BUILD_CONFIGURATION` per build type.

## The risks that mattered

| Risk | What actually happened |
|---|---|
| A JUCE `DialogWindow` on Android has no title bar and no way to close | The audio settings became an in-app overlay with an OK button (wave 4) |
| The system bars came back on rotation | The window is refitted and the bars re-hidden on a 5 Hz timer |
| The NDK build failed on `getEnv` | `JUCE_CORE_INCLUDE_JNI_HELPERS=1` plus a durable NoTitleBar theme |
| A Debug startup assertion on Android | The cause was a non-ASCII UI string literal: the literals stay ASCII-only (a project rule now) |

## What was verified

- The layout at six window sizes (offscreen, no screen access).
- The Android build in Android Studio, and the APK on the device.
- The immersive mode, the rotation refit and the bar autohide on the
  device - these cannot be checked headlessly, so they were checked on
  the tablet.

## What came out of it

- The two reusable skills (`juce-android-window-fit`, `projucer-project`)
  came out of the wave - the recipes are worth more than the app.
- The Projucer-generated Android project became a liability later: the
  hand edits in it could be undone by a resave (wave 16 fixed that).
- The live input (wave 3) was the obvious next material - the tablet has
  a microphone.
